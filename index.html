NoCompile

iOS / macOS 热重载工具，支持 C、C++、Objective-C、Swift。修改代码，保存，1~3 秒内就在运行中的 App 里看到效果，无需等待整个工程重新编译。 Hot reload for iOS and macOS, supporting C, C++, Objective-C and Swift. Edit, save, and see the change in your running app within 1–3 seconds, without rebuilding the whole project.

开始使用Get started

不确定芯片类型？打开「苹果菜单 › 关于本机」，看“芯片”一栏。Not sure which chip you have? Open › About This Mac and check “Chip”.

  1. 下载与本机芯片匹配的 DMG，打开后把 NoCompile 拖进「应用程序」文件夹。Download the DMG for your chip, open it and drag NoCompile into Applications.
  2. 按上面任选一种方式接入客户端，并在 Xcode 里完整构建一次工程。Add the client using one of the options above, then build your project once in Xcode.
  3. 点击菜单栏的 ⚡ 图标 → 选择工程，然后 Command+R 运行 App。Click ⚡ in the menu bar → choose your project, then run the app with Command+R.
  4. 编辑任意源文件并保存，几秒后就能在 App 里看到改动生效。Edit any source file and save. The change shows up in the running app within seconds.
